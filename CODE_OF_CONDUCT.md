# Code of Conduct

## What this is about

This project comes out of club ski racing. The same rules apply here that apply on the hill:
be friendly, help each other out, and don't treat anyone badly because of who they are.

Concretely: we treat each other with respect, regardless of age, origin, gender, sexual
orientation, disability, appearance, religion or experience level. Criticism is aimed at the
work, not the person. Someone asking a question that seems obvious to others gets an answer, not
a put-down.

In particular, the following are not okay: insulting or belittling comments, sexualized language
or advances, harassment inside or outside the project, publishing someone else's private
information without consent, and sustained disruptive behavior.

## Where this applies

Everywhere in the project — issues, pull requests, commit messages, discussions — and whenever
someone represents the project publicly.

## If something happens

Report it to `infos@x-projects.de`. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and, for repeated or serious misconduct, suspend
or permanently exclude someone from contributing. Decisions are explained where possible.

## Attribution

These rules are based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, but are deliberately shorter and in our own words. When in doubt, the spirit of it
matters more than the letter.
